# Third-party references

- Simplified prefecture geometry: https://github.com/dataofjapan/land/blob/master/japan.geojson . Small offshore islands are omitted and Okinawa is placed in an inset for display.
- Fonts: Google Fonts Noto Sans JP / Noto Serif JP, loaded from Google's stylesheet endpoint. The site falls back to system fonts if unavailable.
- Supabase JS client: https://github.com/supabase/supabase-js . Installed through npm with its upstream license.
- Vite: https://github.com/vitejs/vite . Build tooling, upstream license in npm package.
- PGlite: https://github.com/electric-sql/pglite . Development/test tooling only, excluded from the deployed app.
- Regional travel/food/drink reference links are available in the app's backup dialog. Examples are not live opening-hours or restaurant-availability data.
